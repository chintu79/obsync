Why Obsync?

Obsidian Sync costs $4/month and routes your notes through a third-party cloud. Obsync keeps your vault on your own devices — your laptop and your phone talk directly over your network, encrypted, with no account and no subscription. It's free forever, and you can self-host it anywhere.

No cloud

Devices sync directly over your home Wi-Fi or hotspot.

Private

Your notes never leave your devices. Pairing is fingerprint-verified.

Free forever

Open source under MIT / Apache-2.0. No paywall, ever.

Safe

Conflicts are flagged, not clobbered. Snapshots let you roll back.

How it works

Install the app on your laptop.
Run the installer — it starts Obsync automatically and opens the dashboard.
Install the app on your phone.
Download the APK and open it.
Scan the QR code.
Your phone scans the code shown on the laptop's dashboard.
Approve and done.
Tap approve on the laptop. Your vaults stay in sync automatically.

FAQ

Does this need a server or subscription? No. Everything runs on your own devices, on your own network.

Is my data sent anywhere? No. Your notes only travel between your laptop and your phone, encrypted.

What if I edit the same note on both devices? Obsync flags it as a conflict and lets you choose which version to keep.

Can I sync more than a laptop + phone? The server can run on a NAS or Raspberry Pi to serve many devices.
